About Me

I am an AI and computer systems researcher specializing in ML, LLMs, computer architecture, and embedded systems. I obtained my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in Electrical Engineering from Beijing Institute of Technology and the University of Bremen, respectively.

I previously worked as a researcher in the Group of Computer Architecture at the University of Bremen , and in the Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) research department at the German Research Center for Artificial Intelligence (DFKI) . My research focused on applying ML and LLMs to performance modeling and analysis of embedded software, particularly for RISC-V processors.

I am currently seeking opportunities in industry where I can apply my research experience to AI engineering and software development.
